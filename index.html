About me

I'm from Austin, Texas, and I'm a student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. At home it's me, my grandma, my mom, my dad, my little brother Sid, and our dog Shadow.

I got into programming through robotics as a kid, and it stuck. These days I build AI agents that can generate their own user interfaces on the fly, and I've spent time on side projects like a multi-persona chat app, an AI-curated newsletter, and a book-recommendation search engine. At UW I founded Strato VC, the school's first student-run venture club, which raised a $3 million fund to invest in early-stage student startups.

More about my work is at ashwinjandhyala.vercel.app.

A favorite painting

Nighthawks is my favorite painting. I like how much it says with so little: a few people in a lit-up diner, an empty street, and no clear story about who they are or why they're there. It feels calm and a little lonely at the same time.
